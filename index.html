Orca Colab Community

A community plugin and command-line tool that lets coding agents in Orca use your own Google Colab GPU runtimes.

Unofficial community project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. "Google Colab" is a trademark of Google LLC.

What it does

  • Starts, lists and stops Colab runtimes in your own Colab account when you or your agent ask for it.
  • Runs commands on those runtimes and copies files to and from them.
  • Shows your Colab sessions, tasks and estimated compute-unit use in an Orca panel.

How sign-in works

You sign in with Google in your own browser. The resulting tokens are stored only on your computer, in ~/.orca-colab/google-oauth.json, readable only by your user account. There is no Orca Colab Community server; nothing about your account is sent to the project.
