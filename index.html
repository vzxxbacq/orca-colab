What it does

  • Starts, lists and stops Colab runtimes in your own Colab account when you or your agent ask for it.
  • Runs commands on those runtimes and copies files to and from them.
  • Shows your Colab sessions, tasks and estimated compute-unit use in an Orca panel.

How sign-in works

You sign in with Google in your own browser. The resulting tokens are stored only on your computer, in ~/.orca-colab/google-oauth.json, readable only by your user account. The only server involved is a small sign-in relay that adds the app's client secret to Google token requests and stores nothing; nothing about your account is kept by the project.
